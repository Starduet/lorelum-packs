# Sources and provenance

This unreleased Pack distills selected guidance from [GoogleChrome/modern-web-guidance](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/modern-web-guidance) v1.0.0, pinned at commit [`4dd968349e54ca45a05dd5c818fb035c9054b022`](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/modern-web-guidance/tree/4dd968349e54ca45a05dd5c818fb035c9054b022). Individual Practices link to the specific upstream guides supporting their decisions. The Practices are rewritten and organized by decision, not verbatim copies or a complete conversion of the upstream collection. Their examples, emphasis, and compatibility caveats are editorial synthesis; upstream is not represented as having endorsed this Pack.

The upstream repository is distributed under [Apache License 2.0](./LICENSE). Consult the pinned guides and current browser compatibility documentation before relying on time-sensitive platform support. This Pack is not a substitute for testing target browsers and assistive technology.
